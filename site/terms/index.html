What Press is

Press keeps score and works out who owes whom in golf games between friends. No money moves through Press. It records the stakes the players agree and the settlement those stakes produce. You're responsible for following the laws where you play, including any on wagering.

Distances and advice

Yardages, hole maps, hazard notes and club suggestions are estimates, from course maps that may be incomplete or wrong. Check before relying on them, and follow your club's local rules on distance-measuring devices. Advice on where to aim is shown only in practice rounds and when looking a course over, because the Rules of Golf don't allow it in a counted round.
