Delete your Press Golf account

For the Press Golf app on Google Play and the App Store.

How to delete it

In the app

While signed in, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. The screen says what goes and what stays. Enter your password and tap Delete my account.

It needs signal and happens at once. There's no grace period, and it can't be undone.

By email

If you don't have the app or can't sign in, email support@pressgolf.cc from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my Press account". Press replies to confirm, and deletes the account within 30 days.

What's deleted

  • Your account and email address.
  • Your groups and players, rounds and cards, courses (with their greens, maps and hole notes), your bag and usual score, and your ball marks, which your tracked shots come from, including those on someone else's round.
  • Outings you organised, for everyone, including the scores guests typed into them, even if the outing was on someone else's group or course. Guests keep the copy already on their phones.
  • Your own rounds on someone else's group or course, or in their outing. What those were on stays theirs.
  • Your memberships in other people's outings.

What's kept

  • Scores and junk you typed into someone else's outing stay on their card, because they're part of that organiser's round.
  • Someone else's own round or outing on your group or course stays theirs, with their own copy of what it's on. A copy of a course has its tees, greens, maps and hole notes. A copy of a group has only the players they could already see.
  • Server backups age out within 7 days.
  • Service logs (IP address and sign-in times) are deleted automatically within 7 days.

Your phones

  • The phone you delete from goes back to the sign-in screen: your rounds, bag, practice rounds and ball marks on it are erased, and you're signed out. Demo data stays on the phone.
  • Other phones signed in to the account lose access, but keep what's on them until the app is removed. The same goes for every phone after a deletion by email.

Deleting less than your account

You can delete a single round, course, player or club in the app, and it's removed from the server at the next sync.
