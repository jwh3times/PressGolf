Press

Golf games and settling up, for iPhone and Android.

Set the games, enter one scorecard, and Press works out the fewest cash hand-offs at the end of the round: Nassau with presses, skins, junk, Stableford, four-ball, Wolf, Vegas, match play and stroke play. Pops come from each player's Handicap Index and tee.

Contact

Email support@pressgolf.cc. Say which phone you use and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Press opens with demo data you can play with. An account backs up your rounds, moves them to a new phone, and lets you share an outing.

How do other groups join my outing?

Share the outing's code. Each group's scorer joins with it and scores their own card, and the field-wide games settle across every group.

Why is there no yardage on a hole?

Distances need to know where the greens are. In the course editor, find the course on OpenStreetMap, place the greens on the satellite map, or mark each one by standing on it.
