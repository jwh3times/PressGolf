Delete your Press account

Press, by developer name, as on both store listings

In the app

wording while the closed test runs, before in-app deletion ships Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Press shows what will be deleted, then asks for your password. You need signal.

Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone. Afterwards the app on that phone goes back to how it was when first installed, with the demo data.

Without the app

Email support@pressgolf.cc from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my Press account". Press replies to confirm, and deletes the account within 30 days.

What's deleted

  • Your account and email address.
  • Your groups and players, rounds and cards, courses (with their greens, maps and hole notes), your bag and usual score.
  • Outings you organised, including the scores guests typed into them. Guests keep the copy already on their phones.
  • Your memberships in other people's outings.

What's kept

  • Scores you typed into someone else's outing stay on their card, because they're part of that organiser's round.
  • A guest's own round that used your group or course keeps a copy of that group or course, owned by the guest.
  • Server backups age out within 7 days.
  • Service logs (IP address and sign-in times) are deleted automatically within 7 days.

Deleting less than your account

You can delete a single round, course, player or club in the app, and it's removed from the server at the next sync.
